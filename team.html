Leadership & Educators

Meet Our Experienced Professionals

Founder & Director

Allen Tomasello

BCBA M.Ed. Special Education 36+ Yrs Experience

Allen Tomasello is the founder and Director of Discovering Kids Consultation Service and is a Board Certified Behavior Analyst. He received his master's degree in Special Education from Liberty University and has worked with individuals with autism for the past 36 years.

He runs the early intensive behavior intervention program for children birth to 5 years, and provides behavior consultation and training to families, private organizations, and public schools for learners age 5–21. He regularly conducts workshop trainings for staff in verbal behavior, natural environment training, discrete trial instruction, teaching independence, and understanding behavioral function.

Originally from Pennsylvania, he began his career at the Princeton Child Development Institute (PCDI) in New Jersey (founded 1970), and moved to Maine in 1998 when hired by Bancroft NeuroHealth as Director of the Center for Autism Research and Educational Services (CARES).

He has also been a youth baseball and football coach for over 29 years, teaching life skills through goal setting, fair play, and teamwork. He has been married for over 30 years and has ten children with his wife, Cheryl.

Lead Teacher

Erica Hughes

B.A. Psychology MDOE Certified Birth–Gr 8 BHP Certified AAC Partner

Erica holds a Bachelor's Degree in Psychology from the University of Maine and is a Maine-certified Special Education Teacher, Birth–Grade 8. She has completed graduate coursework in Maine Studies, a Certificate in Teaching Deafblind Students, the TouchChat App Partner Program (AAC), and holds Behavioral Health Professional (BHP) certification.

In January, Erica celebrates 10 years with Discovering Kids and 10 years providing ABA-based specially designed instruction. Her experience with children with autism spans 35 years.

She loves finding "the way in" for each child — sometimes a planned strategy, sometimes following a child's interest, sometimes just being a little silly — and believes preschool should be a place where kids feel safe, understood, challenged, and excited to come each day.

Hiring Practices & Staff Qualifications

We take pride in the high standards we employ in hiring Special Educators, Teaching Assistants, Educational Technicians, Behavior Health Professionals, and Registered Behavior Technicians — looking for maturity, compassion, enthusiasm, and willingness to learn as part of a team.

All professionals working with your child are recognized by the Maine Department of Education (MDOE) and/or Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS), with required background checks performed.
