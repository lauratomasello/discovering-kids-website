Family Feedback

Words from Our Community

“

"My child has learned so much since attending Discovering Kids. He has learned how to write his name, is beginning to read, and is learning the importance of manners. He has participated in speech services and has grown so much in his confidence. He has also learned how to put on his own shoes and become more independent.

This program has been absolutely amazing for my child and our family. One of the biggest changes I've noticed is that he has fewer meltdowns and is learning how to use his words when he becomes upset.

My child has also made wonderful friends at Discovering Kids, and he truly loves going every day. The staff at Discovering Kids are incredibly supportive and caring — always willing to listen, answer questions, and make sure families feel heard. Discovering Kids has been the best place for our child, and I am so grateful. I would highly recommend Discovering Kids to any family looking for a supportive, nurturing, encouraging place for their child."

A
Alicia
Parent of Discovering Kids Student

"Discovering Kids has had a profound and positive impact on my family's life. Since becoming involved, my grandchild has made remarkable progress — he is now listening more consistently, following one-step directions, and engaging with the people around him.
